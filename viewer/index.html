A traceable copy of the corrected supplementDownloaded from the U.S. EPA’s CompTox-ExpoCast-httk repository ↗. All 1,352 compound rows and 20 source columns are retained. No deduplication, endpoint imputation, or unit conversion is applied.

Lombardo F, Berellini G, Obach RS. Trend Analysis of a Database of Intravenous Pharmacokinetic Parameters in Humans for 1352 Drug Compounds. Drug Metabolism and Disposition. 2018;46(11):1466–1477.

This is a compound-level compilation, not individual patient data or concentration–time profiles. Consult each record’s reference, comments, and notes for study context and derived measurements. Ionization and MoKa descriptors are source-reported calculations. Blank cells and “n/a” become missing values in the viewer; the workbook preserves the original cells. Displayed numbers are rounded to six significant figures; exports retain source numeric precision. Log plots exclude missing, zero, and negative axis values.
